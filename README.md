# Department of Computer Engineering - Annual Magazine 2025-26
### Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan's Sardar Patel Institute of Technology (S.P.I.T.), Mumbai

This repository contains all assets, source data, generation scripts, and finalized editable documents for the **Computer Engineering Department Annual Magazine (2025-26)**.

---

## 📁 Repository Contents

### 1. Magazine Output Deliverables
- **`CE_Annual_Magazine_2025-26_v2.docx`**: Complete, print-ready, professional DOCX edition featuring stylized section covers, stat callouts, leadership message layouts, vision/mission cards, and editable placeholders.
- **`index.html` & `styles.css`**: Digital interactive web edition of the annual magazine.
- **`generate_magazine.py`**: Python automation script utilizing `python-docx` to compile raw data and image assets into the stylized Word document.

### 2. Extracted Media & Assets
- **`extracted_images/`**: High-resolution image assets extracted from departmental presentations and reports.
- **`Faclty group pic.JPG`** & **`PTM.JPG`**: High-resolution departmental and event photography.

### 3. Departmental Source Data & Reports
- **Curriculum & Overview**: `3&4 Vision,Mission, PO,PSO.docx`, `5. About the Department.docx`, `6. Lab facilities, Smart Classroom.docx`, `7. Overview of Academic Programs.docx`, `8. Faculty and lab Assistant Information.docx`
- **Research & Publications**: `11. Faculty_Students Consultancy, Grant, Patent and Research Publications.docx`
- **Achievements & Projects**: `12. Faculty and Students Achievements.docx`, `13. List of Undergraduate Projects, M.Tech and PhD.docx`
- **Industry & Alumni**: `14. Industry Institute Interaction.docx`, `15 Alumni and Entrepreneurs.docx`
- **Student Activities & Support**: `16. Departmental Activities by Faculty, Students and Associations.docx`, `17. Student Support.docx`, `External Audit Computer Engineering Department 2024-25 and 2025-26.pptx`, `CE_PTM_PPT_30_04_2026.pptx`
- **Reference & Guides**: `Copy of Magazine INDEX.docx`, `Mech-Engg-Dept-Annual-Report-2022-23-Final-28072023.pdf`
